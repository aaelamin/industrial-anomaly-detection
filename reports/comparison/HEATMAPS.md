# Heatmap examples

[Back to the overview](README.md)

These are saved test predictions, shown for seeds 0, 1 and 2. The cyan cross marks the highest-scoring pixel. Each map has its own display range, so compare locations and the printed per-image pixel AP, not brightness across models. Normal images have no defect mask and no per-image AP.

Every category includes its first normal and first defective test image in filename order. Six previously inspected examples are added to connect the gallery to the discussion. This is a disclosed selection, not a representative sample or a new test set. Full category metrics are the main evidence. Images are from [MVTec AD, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://www.mvtec.com/research-teaching/datasets/mvtec-ad).
