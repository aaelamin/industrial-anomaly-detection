Anomalib FastFlow constructor adaptation

vendor/feature_fastflow.py adapts the constructor and retains the CNN forward
and scoring path of anomalib 2.6.2 FastflowModel. Its explicit local weight
loading and audited timm backbone metadata support the study's three CNN routes.
The original source header is retained in the adapter's attribution.
