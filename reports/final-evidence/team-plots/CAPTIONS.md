# Figure captions and interpretation

`main-comparison`: Category-macro image AUROC, pixel AUROC and pixel AP at the fixed endpoint. Each model covers all 15 MVTec categories and three seeds. Error bars are sample SD across seed-level category means; orange marks show those three values. They are not population confidence intervals. All score axes span 0–100%.

`category-changes`: Mean pixel-AP differences across the three seeds for the three planned configuration contrasts. Color is centered at zero with a symmetric range; numeric cells give percentage-point changes. Category-wise patterns are descriptive and not corrected significance tests. The compact fusion pair is matched in flow capacity and representation size. The backbone/recipe contrasts retain the limitations explained in the methods notes.

Both figures use saved verified metrics only. SVG versions contain editable text; PNG versions are supplied for the report and presentation.
